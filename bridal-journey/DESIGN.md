# Bridal Journey visual system

## Mood
Luxury bridal editorial: warm, calm, feminine, refined and spacious. The UI should look commercial and premium, never childish.

## Core tokens
- Page ivory: #FBF7F2
- Secondary ivory: #F8F0E9
- Card: #FFFAF6
- Hairline: #EADBD1
- Main ink: #5C3C35
- Muted text: #9B7F76
- Rose: #AD6758
- Soft rose: #F2DDD6
- Gold accent: #B88D58
- Sage progress: #71816E
- Sage soft: #E8EEE7

Use gold sparingly. Use sage for completion/progress. Use rose for identity and active states.

## Typography
Arabic body: IBM Plex Sans Arabic or a similarly clean Arabic sans.
Display/logo: Aref Ruqaa or another refined Arabic display face.
English supporting lines: a restrained serif is allowed.

## Desktop composition
1. Header 80–90px high
2. Hero: image on the left, editorial title + two KPI cards on the right
3. Five equal summary cards
4. Three panels below:
   - weekly focus
   - appointments
   - budget
5. Generous outer padding and low-contrast borders

## Hero
- Rounded outer container: 26–30px
- Bride image should occupy roughly 50–55% of hero width on large screens
- Copy area should remain airy
- Two KPI cards sit under tagline
- Do not overlay dense text on the photo

## Cards
- Radius 16–22px
- Thin warm border
- Very soft shadow only
- No dark drop shadows
- Compact vertical rhythm

## Mobile
- Bottom navigation for the real product
- Cards stack intentionally
- Keep progress and budget readable without horizontal scrolling
- Touch targets >= 44px for real controls

## Motion
120–280ms soft ease-out.
Use motion to confirm completion, tab changes and progress.
Honor prefers-reduced-motion.

## Non-negotiable
Compare every major visual change against `assets/reference-tiny.jpg`. If a redesign is prettier but moves away from the reference identity, do not use it.
