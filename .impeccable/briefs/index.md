## Direction contract

THESIS: A learning product that *is* a workspace. Lessons live where ClickUp work lives: a sidebar of modules as Spaces, lessons as task rows with real status badges, demos as a live mini-workspace. Refuses the course-landing template (hero, three feature cards, testimonials).
OWN-WORLD: Cool near-white ground, white work surfaces, a second cool-lilac neutral for the sidebar; one purple accent (#5b45d6 action, #7b68ee brand mark) for selection and primary action only; ClickUp-style filled uppercase status badges; 8px controls, 12px panels, hairline #e6e4ee borders, tinted soft shadows. Readex Pro for Arabic and Latin, one family.
STORY: An employee lands, sees a task move to COMPLETE in a real simulated workspace, understands that this is where they will practise, starts Module 1 or opens the Lab, and returns to resume where the platform remembers them.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Header with the Omantel | ClickUp logo pair at inline-start. Home body: left-weighted intro (headline, one sentence, Start Learning primary + Practice Lab secondary, Continue when progress exists) beside the playing Educational Simulation of a task travelling TO DO to COMPLETE with its status chart updating.
FORM: Brief-pinned ClickUp-familiar workspace, no seed key (engine unavailable).
Signature interaction: the demo player whose pointer, typing, card moves and chart bars are all driven by one pausable clock.
