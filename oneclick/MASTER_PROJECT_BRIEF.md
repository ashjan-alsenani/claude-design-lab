# One Click: Master Project Brief

Permanent project brief, saved from the owner's instructions of 2026-10-02. Content is kept
complete; list formatting is condensed. When this brief and code disagree, the brief wins
unless DECISIONS.md records an approved change.

## 1. Most important operating rule
The owner has a full-time job and very limited time. **Minimize her work, maximize Claude's.**
Claude takes ownership of execution and does not ask small questions (colors, fonts, frameworks,
libraries, databases, button/card styles, animation libraries, folder structures, patterns,
testing libraries, icon libraries, architecture). Claude researches, decides and documents.
Owner is needed only for: account creation, identity verification, legal business information,
banking information, passwords, API keys, permissions, paid subscription approval, domain purchase
approval, production authorization, irreversible actions, major legal or financial decisions.
When needed, write **OWNER ACTION REQUIRED** with: why, exactly what, where to click, what to
enter, what to send back, cost, whether it blocks development.

## 2. Do not stop at planning
Planning only where it helps execution. Create structure, docs, decisions, then build.

## 3. Brand name (locked)
**One Click** (owner decision 2026-10-02; originally written "OneClick", briefly "One Click Digital Hub"). Products use "One Click + Product" (e.g. Bride Planner). No renaming or naming exercises. Before commercial launch:
check domains, social handles, confusingly similar brands, obvious trademark risks; flag serious
conflicts but never rename independently.

## 4. Brand meaning
Making everyday life easier through simple, beautiful and useful digital solutions: less effort,
less complexity, saved time, organization, smart convenience, human technology, chaos to clarity.
Feeling: "Something that used to take effort just became much easier."

## 5. Personality
Modern, smart, helpful, friendly, premium, creative, simple, colorful, human, trustworthy,
practical, clean, international, organized, positive, visually beautiful. Not childish, cheap,
overly corporate, complicated, cold, generic, dropshipping, template shop, random AI startup.

## 6. Positioning and slogan
Territory: life made simpler; smart tools, simpler life; less effort, more life; simplify everyday
life. Deliver: brand statement, primary slogan, secondary messaging, elevator pitch, Instagram bio,
website hero, Arabic + English. Do not keep changing the slogan once approved.

## 7. Business vision
International digital-products company from the Sultanate of Oman for Oman, KSA, UAE, Kuwait,
Qatar, Bahrain, other GCC and worldwide. Must feel like a genuine international digital lifestyle
and productivity company, not "someone sells PDFs".

## 8. Business model
A) Ready-made interactive products (mini web apps, interactive sites, dashboards, organizers,
trackers, planners, calculators, smart checklists, tools), e.g. Bride, Grocery, Daily/Weekly
Planner, Time Management, Workout, Meal, Travel, Budget, Savings, Study, Student, Habit, Home
Cleaning, Ramadan, Event, Family, Gift, Small Business, Project, Goal, Self-Care, Checklists.
Each solves a real problem; not the same template with different words.
B) Downloadable products: templates, planners, PDFs, checklists, worksheets, guides, kits,
bundles, printables, resources.
C) Custom solutions: websites, mini apps, tools, interactive planners, dashboards, organizers.
All under one One Click brand.

## 9. Product experience
Dashboards, checklists, tasks, notes, calendars, timelines, progress, charts, statistics,
reminders, categories, search, filters, favorites, calculators, exports, printable views,
settings, sharing, onboarding. Every feature needs a clear user benefit.

## 10. Product framework
Build a reusable **One Click Product Framework** (auth, dashboard shell, navigation, tasks,
checklists, notes, calendar, charts, progress, search, filters, categories, export, settings,
localization, themes, analytics, entitlements, notifications, responsive layouts). Shared
foundations, unique experiences.

## 11. First products
Start with a few strong products. Evaluate Bride, Grocery, Planner, Fit, Study, Travel, Budget:
problem, audience, benefit, MVP, premium features, differentiator, reusable components, effort,
marketing opportunity, risks, expansion. Choose by value and reuse.

## 12. Naming
"One Click + Product" unless a clearly better architecture appears; always tied to One Click.

## 13-15. Product examples
Bride: countdown, timeline, budget, guests, vendors, tasks, appointments, dress, shopping,
inspiration, notes, events, gifts, seating, checklist, progress. Focused MVP first.
Grocery: reusable lists, categories, quantity, expected prices, purchased status, estimated
total, stores, favorites, recurring, household lists. Fast.
Fitness: schedule, exercise library, sets/reps, progress, statistics. No medical claims or
diagnosis; wellness disclaimers.

## 16-17. Identity and logo
Concept, positioning, personality, story, slogan, messaging, primary + secondary logo, wordmark,
icon mark, favicon, social profile icon, light/dark/monochrome, editable SVG. Color system:
primary, secondary, accent, background, surface, text, muted, success, warning, error, info.
Colorful but elegant, no random rainbow. Logo communicates simplicity, ease, flow, completion,
convenience, connection, transformation. Avoid cursor, cursor-in-circle, power icon, lightning,
meaningless tech symbol, cheap gradient. Must work as logo, favicon, Instagram avatar, app icon.

## 18-19. Typography and design system
Premium Arabic and English: display, heading, body, numbers, buttons, labels; test both.
Design system: colors, type, icons, illustrations, photography, gradients, borders, radius,
shadows, spacing, grid, buttons, forms, cards, tables, charts, modals, toasts, navigation,
states, animation. Maintain BRAND_GUIDELINES.md.

## 20-22. Website direction, storytelling, animation
Not a Shopify theme, dropshipping store, generic SaaS, AI landing page, template marketplace or
repetitive card grid. Premium, visual: smooth animation, micro-interactions, animated icons,
original illustrations, mockups, interactive previews, counters, progress, before/after,
scroll storytelling, soft gradients, screenshots, floating elements, branded graphics.
Story pattern: Problem → Chaos → One Click → Clear Result, everywhere (site, ads, reels, demos,
landing pages). Motion must aid understanding or delight; respect prefers-reduced-motion;
performance first.

## 23. Homepage
Explains One Click in seconds (what, sells, who, why, action). Hero, animated preview, popular
products, categories, how One Click helps, problem → solution, interactive demos, how it works,
benefits, collections, new products, custom solutions, education, genuine testimonials when
available, FAQ, newsletter, final CTA. **No fake numbers, reviews, downloads, revenue, awards,
clients.** Honest launch messaging.

## 24-26. Categories, product pages, pricing
Scalable, not hard-coded categories with subcategories, tags, multiple categories.
Product page: hero, mockup, explanation, problem, audience, benefits, features, screenshots,
demo, what's included, devices, languages, FAQs, price, CTA, related, terms, license, support,
safe previews, and clearly whether lifetime, subscription, limited, download, interactive,
bundle or custom. Never mislead. Price ~15 OMR typical but editable; support per-product prices,
bundles, discounts, free, premium, subscriptions, campaign pricing, multi-currency.

## 27-31. Oman base, payments, compliance, currency
Oman is the home jurisdiction; GCC + international scalability.
**Payment: do not select or integrate any gateway yet.** Owner will consult her bank. Build a
provider-independent payment architecture with placeholders, modular checkout, no card data,
a test checkout clearly marked **PAYMENT PROVIDER NOT YET CONNECTED**, ready order/purchase/
entitlement architecture; connect the chosen gateway later without rebuilding. Never claim
payment is live before it is.
Business settings in Admin (legal name, CR, VAT, address, support email/phone, terms, policies),
not public until provided. Maintain OMAN_LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md using current official sources
(MOCIIP, Oman Business Platform, Tax Authority, consumer/e-commerce rules): CR, licensed activity,
e-commerce, website and social selling/marketing, Ma'roof Oman, consumer info, terms, refunds,
privacy, tax/VAT, e-invoicing. No definitive legal conclusions; flag accountant/lawyer/bank/
authority confirmations; tax config editable in Admin, thresholds not hard-coded.
Currency: OMR base; future SAR, AED, KWD, QAR, BHD, USD, EUR, GBP; no homemade conversion.

## 32-34. Languages, mobile, international branding
Arabic and English first-class (RTL/LTR) from the start; natural, Gulf-friendly Arabic where
appropriate, formal Arabic for legal/support; every major screen tested in both; extensible
to more languages. Mobile-first for Instagram traffic: fast, comfortable targets, vertical demos,
excellent Arabic mobile. Visually international, Oman as trusted home base, global-friendly
domain, optional Oman domain/redirect later.

## 35. Owner workload (Oman override)
Claude does everything technically possible; owner only for credentials, account creation and
verification, legal/bank info, paid approvals, important business decisions, production auth.

## 36-37. Accounts and access
Register, log in, reset password, profile, purchased products, web product access, downloads,
orders, favorites, support, settings. Proper authorization; never protect paid content by hidden
URLs. Interactive products via authenticated entitlements; downloads via secure authorized links;
future personal/commercial/team licenses; Digital Product License page.

## 38. Custom solution request
Smart brief: goal, type, audience, features, references, languages, deadline, budget, branding,
payment needs, attachments, notes. Statuses: New, Reviewing, Need Information, Quoted, Accepted,
In Progress, Review, Completed, Closed. Save lead, confirm to customer, notify owner, CRM-ready.

## 39-40. Admin and Business Command Center
Secure simple Admin, no SQL: products, categories, tags, prices, content, featured, screenshots,
downloads, customers, orders, purchases, entitlements, discounts, custom requests, reviews,
testimonials, FAQs, guides, announcements, newsletter, support, social content, campaigns,
videos, business settings. Owner dashboard: revenue, orders, customers, popular products, views,
traffic, conversion, Instagram, marketing, requests, support, content awaiting approval, activity,
alerts; highlights what needs attention.

## 41-42. Content hub and SEO
Genuinely helpful guides/ideas/tips/how-to/inspiration/resources (wedding, productivity, grocery,
travel, study, budget, organization, digital habits); no low-quality mass AI articles.
Technical SEO: titles, descriptions, clean URLs, sitemap, robots, canonical, Open Graph, social
images, product metadata, Organization/Product/FAQ/Article/Breadcrumb structured data, hreflang;
Arabic and English; search intent; no keyword stuffing.

## 43-44. Instagram
Core channel. Profile image, bio, highlight categories and covers, post/carousel/story/reel
styles, launch and educational templates, 30-day launch plan mixing tips, demos, relatable
problems, before/after, tutorials, walkthroughs, launches, behind the scenes, questions, offers,
education; not all "Buy now"; Arabic, English, bilingual. Research the official Meta API when
implementing; social content queue (product, campaign, media, caption, CTA, language, type, link,
UTM, schedule, approval, publishing status); Draft → Review → Approved → Scheduled → Published;
owner approval by default; auto-publish only when intentionally approved; safe failure handling;
never silently lose scheduled content.

## 45-50. Video
When asked for any video, choose tools automatically (never ask which program). Priority:
free/included first; motion graphics tools (e.g. HyperFrames) for UI/product demos; generative AI
video only when people/lifestyle/cinematic footage is truly needed; editing tools for voiceover,
captions, sound, music, timing, export. Cost control: free → included → open-source → low-cost →
pay-as-you-go → subscription; state expected cost before spending meaningful credits.
Default: vertical 9:16, Reel-compatible, mobile-first, hook in 1-3 s, clear story, professional
motion, readable subtitles, brand colors and fonts, one message, strong CTA, One Click end screen,
not overcrowded. Full workflow: concept, hook, script, scenes, storyboard, shot list, screenshots,
assets, animation, voiceover plan, captions, timing, CTA, music, end screen, cover, export.
Per major product, a marketing package: summary, audience, problem, benefit, hooks, headlines,
CTAs, caption, reel, story, carousel, ad, demo idea, visual direction, landing messaging; create
the assets when tools allow.

## 51-58. Links, email, automation, referrals, promotions, favorites, support, reviews
Campaign-specific UTM links to relevant pages; mobile social landing pages. Transactional email
(welcome, verification, purchase, access, reset, custom request, support), branded templates,
consent-based marketing with unsubscribe, no hard-coded personal credentials. Automation
foundations (welcome, launches, onboarding, feedback, cross-sell, coupons, Ramadan, Eid, wedding
season, back-to-school, travel, New Year) without spam. Future referral/affiliate architecture
(not an MVP blocker). Promotions: coupons, percent/fixed, product-specific, expiry, max uses,
bundles, launch offers, cross/upsells, no dark patterns. Favorites: secure for logged-in, local
for guests. Support center: help, FAQ, access/account help, payment help placeholder, contact
with order/product reference, statuses. Reviews: Verified Purchase, moderation, never fake,
never hide legitimate negative reviews.

## 59-62. Analytics, privacy, legal, security
Privacy-conscious analytics with an event dictionary (visits, product views, demo interaction,
signup, checkout start, purchase, custom request, newsletter, campaign source, usage); no
unnecessary data. Consent, cookie controls, third-party documentation, retention principles,
customer privacy controls; no compliance claims without professional confirmation. Drafts for
Terms, Privacy, Cookies, Refunds, Digital Product License, Custom Service Terms, Acceptable Use,
IP, Contact; digital-product specific; flag legal review. Security: secure auth, authorization,
admin roles, server-side validation, rate limiting, spam protection, secure cookies, CSRF where
relevant, sanitization, HTTPS, secret management, env vars, dependency checks; never expose or
commit secrets; .env.example; SECURITY.md.

## 63-65. Stack, database, content
Modern maintainable stack (Next.js, React, TypeScript, modern styling, animation, Supabase when
appropriate, GitHub, Vercel or equivalent, browser testing) chosen on merit with current docs;
no unnecessary microservices or complexity. Proper data model (users, profiles, products,
versions, categories, tags, pricing, orders, purchases, payment events, entitlements, downloads,
favorites, reviews, custom requests, support, discounts, newsletter, social content, campaigns,
video assets, business settings) with migrations and documented relationships; no manual
production DB edits. Sensible content management; avoid a heavy CMS unless valuable.

## 66-69. Mobile, accessibility, performance, visual assets
Test iPhone, Android, small screens, navigation, forms, login, product pages, demos, dashboard,
admin, Arabic RTL, English LTR; app-like products; PWA if valuable. WCAG 2.2 AA principles:
semantic HTML, keyboard, focus, labels, alt text, contrast, screen readers, reduced motion, not
color-only. Optimize images, video, fonts, JS, rendering, animation, lazy loading, Core Web
Vitals. Prefer original graphics, SVG, UI mockups, screenshots, branded shapes; verify licenses.

## 70-75. Automation, journey, conversion, lead magnet, testing, review
Automate safe repetitive work (delivery, access, emails, orders, lead notifications, social
scheduling, reporting, analytics, backups, publishing); never irreversible high-risk actions
without approval. Journey: Instagram/Search → Landing → Discovery → Product → Demo → Checkout →
Payment → Confirmation → Access → Dashboard → Onboarding → Usage → Support → Review → Related.
Clear CTAs, understandable pricing, no dark patterns, only true security/payment claims. Optional
free resource, no aggressive popups. Automated tests (auth, authorization, forms, accounts,
access, entitlements, admin permissions, Arabic/English, RTL/LTR, mobile/desktop, errors,
expired sessions, links, accessibility, performance, mock checkout, webhooks) with Playwright.
Before "complete": run, test, console, server logs, mobile, desktop, Arabic, English,
permissions, edge cases.

## 76-78. Design review, errors, backups
Review every major page like a senior creative director (generic? AI-looking? crowded?
hierarchy? typography? color? motion? Arabic parity? mobile?) and improve before presenting.
No raw technical errors to customers; professional loading, empty, error, 404, network, form and
permission states; log technical errors. Backup strategy for database, files, code, config;
disaster recovery; not dependent on one laptop.

## 79-81. Git, owner actions, costs
Clear commits, no secrets. Maintain MASTER_PROJECT_BRIEF.md, PROJECT_STATUS.md, DECISIONS.md,
OWNER_ACTIONS.md (NOW / LATER / OPTIONAL with reason, instructions, cost, what to send back),
LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md, OMAN_LAUNCH_CHECKLIST.md, SECURITY.md, DEPLOYMENT.md, BRAND_GUIDELINES.md,
COSTS.md (mandatory / optional / future scaling), CHANGELOG.md, README.md. Never purchase or
activate paid services without explicit approval.

## 82-85. Tools, honesty, status labels, external services
Use connected tools proactively; owner describes outcomes, Claude picks tools. Never fake tool
access: state what is prepared, mocked, waiting for connection or owner permission. Status labels:
PLANNED, DESIGNED, BUILT LOCALLY, TESTED, MOCK CONNECTED, SANDBOX CONNECTED, PRODUCTION CONNECTION
REQUIRES OWNER, LIVE (only if truly live). Before recommending a service: need, free tier,
pricing, Oman/GCC/international support, commercial restrictions, compatibility, lock-in,
cheaper alternatives.

## 86-92. Summary, scalability, AI, roadmap, initial build, demo, objective
Prepare weekly owner summary architecture. Do not block future mobile apps, subscriptions,
membership, bundles, team licenses, affiliates, gift cards, languages, payment providers, AI,
creator marketplace, corporate products. AI only when useful; protect privacy; no sensitive data
to AI without authorization and disclosure. Roadmap phases 1-23 (foundation → identity → design
system → architecture → homepage → catalog → product pages → AR/EN → auth → dashboard → framework
→ first products → admin → custom request → content hub → marketing → video → testing → Oman
review → payments later → soft launch → public launch → optimization). Initial build: identity
foundation, homepage, catalog, categories, product page, Arabic + English, RTL/LTR, auth
foundation, dashboard foundation, custom solution page, About, guides, help/FAQ, contact, legal
framework, admin foundation, payment abstraction only, mobile nav, footer, SEO, analytics, social
and video/content architecture. Demo data clearly marked; never fabricate sales, reviews, counts.
Objective: a technology-powered international digital lifestyle brand from Oman, associated with
simple, useful, smart, beautiful, easy, reliable, helpful; built for hundreds of products.

## 93-95. Execution and reporting
Take ownership; research, decide, build, test, fix, optimize, protect, keep costs low, automate.
Report concisely: **COMPLETED**, **OWNER ACTION REQUIRED** (only if needed), **NEXT**.
No naming exercise, no payment provider, no tool-choice or small design questions.
